Privacy Policy

TodayI • Effective date: 2025-12-30

This Privacy Policy explains how TodayI (“the App”) collects, uses, and shares information when you use the app.

Information We Collect

  • Account information: email address and/or Apple ID sign-in identifiers (if you create an account).
  • User content: journal entries (“memories”), mood selections, comments, and media you choose to upload (photos/videos/links).
  • Usage data: basic diagnostics and app performance information (to improve reliability).

How We Use Information

  • To provide core features (saving and syncing your memories, showing your feed, enabling comments).
  • To maintain security (authentication, fraud prevention, and abuse mitigation).
  • To improve the app (debugging, performance monitoring).

How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with service providers that help operate the app, such as:

  • Firebase (Google): authentication and data storage/sync (e.g., Firestore, Firebase Auth).
  • Apple: Sign in with Apple and App Store billing, if used.

Public vs Private Content

Some content may be visible to others depending on your in-app privacy settings. If you mark a memory as public, it may appear in the global feed and be viewable by other users. Private memories are intended to be visible only to you.

Data Retention

We retain your information as long as necessary to provide the app or comply with legal obligations. You may delete content within the app. Account deletion requests may be supported depending on the app’s features.

Your Choices

  • You can update privacy settings for memories (public/private) in the app.
  • You can block other users in the global feed (blocked users are hidden from your feed).
  • You can request help via the contact method below.

Security

We use reasonable safeguards to protect information. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

Children

The app is not intended for children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country) without parental consent.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will update the effective date above when changes are posted.
